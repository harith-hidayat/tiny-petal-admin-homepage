Privacy Policy

Effective 8 November 2026 · version 1

1. Who we are

This notice is given under section 7 of the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA). Tiny Petal ("we") provides the Tiny Petal Admin app ("the App") and is the data controller for your account data.

Each company using the App is the data controller for the personal data it enters about its own customers. We process that data on the company's behalf. If you are a customer of such a company, contact that company about your data.

2. The personal data we process

Account: your name, email address and password (stored only as a secure hash by our sign-in provider), the company you join, and your role and access in it.

Business records your company enters: its customers' names, phone numbers and addresses, event details and dates, orders, payments, payment proofs (photos and files), documents, notes, and the people named in its ledger.

Activity: a record of actions in a company (who did what, when, and what changed), and when you accepted these documents.

On your phone: your App settings (theme, notifications, which pages lock) stay on the phone. We do not collect your location, contacts or advertising ID.

Fingerprint or face: when you unlock the App with your phone's lock, your phone checks it and tells the App only whether it passed. Your biometric data never leaves your phone, and we never receive it.

We get this data from you, from the members of your company, and from your company about its customers.

3. Why we use it

To create your account and let you join a company; to record orders and payments and make invoices, receipts and other documents; to keep the App and each company's data secure (sign-in, the phone lock, the audit trail); to show reminders and update notices; to answer your requests; to keep the App working well; and to meet our legal obligations.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use it for advertising or direct marketing.

4. What you must give

Your name, email address and password are needed to create an account. Without them we cannot provide the App. Anything else is up to you, but some features may not work without it.

5. Who sees it

Members of your company, as far as the owner's access settings allow.

Service providers who process data for us under security obligations: Supabase (database, sign-in and file storage, in Singapore), and Google, only if your company connects Google Drive (its files then go to that Drive).

Google Drive, if your company's owner connects it: the App asks Google only for access to the files the App itself creates in that Drive and for the account's email address. It uses this access only to save and restore your company's documents, payment proofs and backups, and it cannot see your other files. The App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or let anyone read it except with your permission, for security, or where the law requires.

Professional advisers, and authorities or courts where the law requires. If the App is transferred to another business, the data may move with it under this Policy.

6. Transfers outside Malaysia

The App's data is stored on servers in Singapore, and Google Drive may store files in other countries. By accepting this Policy you consent to these transfers. We transfer data only as section 129 of the PDPA allows, and we require our providers to protect it.

7. How we protect it

Data travels encrypted (HTTPS). The database lets each person see only their own company's data, as its owner allows. Passwords are hashed, the App can lock behind your phone's lock, and actions are recorded in an audit trail. No system is perfectly secure, so keep your password and phone safe.

8. If something goes wrong

If a personal data breach happens, we will notify the Personal Data Protection Commissioner as the law requires, and tell affected people without unnecessary delay where it is likely to cause them significant harm. Where the data is a company's, we will tell that company so it can act.

9. How long we keep it

We keep account data while your account is open. A company's records are kept while it uses the App and for as long as it needs them for its legal and accounting obligations (Malaysian law usually requires business records for 7 years). Then they are deleted or anonymised. Backups are replaced over time.

10. Your rights

Under the PDPA you may ask to see the personal data we hold about you, have it corrected, withdraw your consent, ask us to stop processing that is likely to cause you damage or distress, and ask for your data to be sent to you or another controller (data portability). Withdrawing consent may mean we can no longer provide the App to you.

Email admin.tinypetal@gmail.com. We will reply within 21 days, as the PDPA requires, and may charge a fee only where the law permits.

11. Deleting your account

Email admin.tinypetal@gmail.com from your account's email address. We will delete your account and your personal data, except what your company or the law requires us to keep. Records you entered for your company stay with that company.

12. Age

The App is for adults working in a business. It is not meant for anyone under 18.

13. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy. The version and its effective date are shown at the top. When a change matters, the App will ask you to accept the new version before you carry on.

14. Language

This Policy is given in English and Bahasa Malaysia, as the PDPA requires. If the two differ, the English version prevails.

15. Contact

Tiny Petal · admin.tinypetal@gmail.com

1. Siapa kami

Notis ini diberikan di bawah seksyen 7 Akta Perlindungan Data Peribadi 2010 (PDPA). Tiny Petal ("kami") menyediakan aplikasi Tiny Petal Admin ("Aplikasi") dan ialah pengawal data bagi data akaun anda.

Setiap syarikat yang menggunakan Aplikasi ialah pengawal data bagi data peribadi yang dimasukkannya mengenai pelanggannya sendiri. Kami memproses data tersebut bagi pihak syarikat itu. Jika anda pelanggan syarikat sedemikian, hubungi syarikat itu mengenai data anda.

2. Data peribadi yang kami proses

Akaun: nama, alamat e-mel dan kata laluan anda (disimpan hanya sebagai cincangan selamat oleh penyedia log masuk kami), syarikat yang anda sertai, serta peranan dan akses anda di dalamnya.

Rekod perniagaan yang dimasukkan syarikat anda: nama, nombor telefon dan alamat pelanggannya, butiran dan tarikh acara, pesanan, bayaran, bukti bayaran (gambar dan fail), dokumen, nota, dan orang yang dinamakan dalam lejarnya.

Aktiviti: rekod tindakan dalam syarikat (siapa melakukan apa, bila, dan apa yang berubah), serta bila anda menerima dokumen ini.

Di telefon anda: tetapan Aplikasi anda (tema, pemberitahuan, halaman yang dikunci) kekal di telefon. Kami tidak mengumpul lokasi, kenalan atau ID pengiklanan anda.

Cap jari atau wajah: apabila anda membuka kunci Aplikasi dengan kunci telefon, telefon anda yang menyemaknya dan hanya memberitahu Aplikasi sama ada ia lulus. Data biometrik anda tidak pernah meninggalkan telefon anda, dan kami tidak pernah menerimanya.

Kami memperoleh data ini daripada anda, daripada ahli syarikat anda, dan daripada syarikat anda mengenai pelanggannya.

3. Tujuan kami menggunakannya

Untuk membuka akaun anda dan membolehkan anda menyertai syarikat; merekod pesanan dan bayaran serta menghasilkan invois, resit dan dokumen lain; memastikan Aplikasi dan data setiap syarikat selamat (log masuk, kunci telefon, jejak audit); memaparkan peringatan dan notis kemas kini; menjawab permintaan anda; memastikan Aplikasi berfungsi dengan baik; dan memenuhi kewajipan undang-undang kami.

Kami tidak menjual data peribadi, dan tidak menggunakannya untuk pengiklanan atau pemasaran langsung.

4. Apa yang wajib anda berikan

Nama, alamat e-mel dan kata laluan anda diperlukan untuk membuka akaun. Tanpanya kami tidak dapat menyediakan Aplikasi. Selain itu terpulang kepada anda, tetapi sesetengah ciri mungkin tidak berfungsi tanpanya.

5. Siapa yang melihatnya

Ahli syarikat anda, setakat yang dibenarkan oleh tetapan akses pemilik.

Penyedia perkhidmatan yang memproses data bagi pihak kami di bawah kewajipan keselamatan: Supabase (pangkalan data, log masuk dan storan fail, di Singapura), dan Google, hanya jika syarikat anda menyambungkan Google Drive (failnya kemudian disimpan dalam Drive tersebut).

Google Drive, jika pemilik syarikat anda menyambungkannya: Aplikasi hanya meminta akses Google kepada fail yang dicipta oleh Aplikasi sendiri dalam Drive tersebut dan kepada alamat e-mel akaun itu. Akses ini digunakan hanya untuk menyimpan dan memulihkan dokumen, bukti pembayaran dan sandaran syarikat anda, dan Aplikasi tidak dapat melihat fail anda yang lain. Penggunaan dan pemindahan maklumat yang diterima daripada API Google oleh Aplikasi mematuhi Dasar Data Pengguna Perkhidmatan API Google (Google API Services User Data Policy), termasuk keperluan Penggunaan Terhad (Limited Use). Kami tidak menjualnya, menggunakannya untuk pengiklanan, atau membenarkan sesiapa membacanya kecuali dengan kebenaran anda, untuk keselamatan, atau apabila dikehendaki undang-undang.

Penasihat profesional, dan pihak berkuasa atau mahkamah apabila dikehendaki undang-undang. Jika Aplikasi dipindahkan kepada perniagaan lain, data boleh berpindah bersamanya di bawah Dasar ini.

6. Pemindahan ke luar Malaysia

Data Aplikasi disimpan di pelayan di Singapura, dan Google Drive mungkin menyimpan fail di negara lain. Dengan menerima Dasar ini anda bersetuju dengan pemindahan tersebut. Kami memindahkan data hanya sebagaimana dibenarkan oleh seksyen 129 PDPA, dan kami mewajibkan penyedia kami melindunginya.

7. Cara kami melindunginya

Data dihantar secara tersulit (HTTPS). Pangkalan data hanya membenarkan setiap orang melihat data syarikatnya sendiri, sebagaimana dibenarkan pemiliknya. Kata laluan dicincang, Aplikasi boleh dikunci dengan kunci telefon anda, dan tindakan direkodkan dalam jejak audit. Tiada sistem yang selamat sepenuhnya, jadi jaga kata laluan dan telefon anda.

Jika berlaku pelanggaran data peribadi, kami akan memaklumkan Pesuruhjaya Perlindungan Data Peribadi sebagaimana dikehendaki undang-undang, dan memaklumkan orang yang terjejas tanpa kelewatan yang tidak perlu jika ia mungkin menyebabkan kemudaratan yang ketara kepada mereka. Kami menyimpan data akaun selagi akaun anda dibuka.
